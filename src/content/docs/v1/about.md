EasyTidy is an open-source, professional file management tool designed to provide efficient and convenient file classification and organization. Through custom settings, users can achieve automatic organization, scheduled organization, real-time organization, and manual organization, meeting file management needs across various scenarios. Crafted with ♥ by its author.

If you find that DropIT's task monitoring doesn't meet your needs, or the price of File Juggler 3 is not quite suitable, you can give EasyTidy a try. EasyTidy is currently under active development, and we welcome your suggestions. Reasonable feature requests will be considered and accepted.
